## Claims and text

- "Spin-singlet" → the ³P₀ state with C = diag(+1, +1, −1).
- Removed "model-independent" and "constitutes a test of nonlocality in the strict sense that Bell's theorem requires".
- Removed the argument that existing measurements leave a timelike-signalling loophole; v2 cites Belle II (Ehataht et al.) for the spacelike separation of relativistic pairs and frames the novelty as resolving the separation and bounding v_ψ.
- Removed the 5% timelike paragraph ("internal consistency check").
- "Quantum states represent behavior established outside of spacetime or via superluminal connection" → statement of Bell's theorem.
- The v_ψ bound is stated in the laboratory frame, with the note that it holds in frames moving slowly relative to it, including the CMB frame.
- Added: Bancal et al. (Nature Phys. 8, 867), Yin et al. (PRL 110, 260407; 1.38 × 10⁴ c), FCC Feasibility Study Vols. 1–2, CLD, Li et al. (σ_ZH), YR4.
- Added: backgrounds are not simulated; assumptions of the detector model listed in the text.
- m12 is no longer used as a result; the text explains why (plug-in estimator biased at these sample sizes).
- Figures replaced: acoplanarity with post-selection SM expectation; v_min response and purity; expected exclusion with bands.
- Extensions list: Z→ee and Z→qq̄ are now in the analysis; beam polarisation is attributed to linear colliders only.

## Assumptions (not from a cited document)

- σ(z0) equal to σ(d0); track-angle multiple-scattering term 1 mrad GeV.
- Photon acceptance |cos θ| < 0.99, threshold 0.5 GeV; π ν identification fails with a photon > 1 GeV within 0.1 rad of the pion; FSR recovery cone 0.1 rad.
- Tracking acceptance |cos θ| < 0.98; pions above 1 GeV.
- Hadronic Z: two jets along the Z decay quarks with 5% energy resolution each and exact directions; primary-vertex resolution as in the leptonic case.
- No backgrounds; no beamstrahlung beyond the energy spread.
